KaRama IT — design research and direction
Updated 14 October 2026

References reviewed for the initial design
https://linear.app/ — focused positioning and concrete feature storytelling.
https://vercel.com/ — technical capabilities and clear calls to action.
https://www.thoughtworks.com/ — business-led consultancy narratives.

Brand palette
The supplied horizontal PNG contains bright blue pixels centred around RGB
(0,149,253). Primary blue is #0095fd; lighter blue #65bcff supports readable text
on dark surfaces. Secondary orange #fd6700 reflects the logo's warm nodes.
Dark navy and pale blue supporting surfaces replace the original olive palette.
Both original logos are preserved unchanged on light backing panels.

Structure
Five separate HTML documents: Home, Expertise, Possibilities, Our Approach,
and Contact. Shared header/footer navigation, current-page indication, dedicated
titles and descriptions. The home page introduces and links to the inner pages.
Service links open the relevant expertise tab; service enquiry links preselect
the same service on Contact. Business possibilities are illustrative, not case studies.

Contact
The contact form's Submit button opens the visitor's email app with the enquiry
addressed to info@karama.tech. Contact details are those supplied by the owner. Service copy remains subject
to the owner's confirmation before a public launch.

Accessibility and performance
Semantic landmarks, skip link, visible focus states, native dialog, keyboard service
tabs, mobile menu, reduced motion and local system fonts/assets. No build required.
