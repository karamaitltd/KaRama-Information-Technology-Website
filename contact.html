A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Where should I start?+

Start with the challenge you want to solve. The project brief helps you describe your goals, the services you’re interested in and your preferred timeline.

Can a project begin with a smaller scope?+

A focused first phase can be a sensible starting point. Define the essentials, agree on what success looks like and identify what can follow later.

What information is useful for a first conversation?+

Your business goals, current website or systems, main challenges, approximate budget and any important dates. You don’t need a technical specification to start.
